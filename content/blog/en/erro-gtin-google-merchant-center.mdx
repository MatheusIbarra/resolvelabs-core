Products rejected in Google Merchant Center because of the GTIN are a common problem in online stores, and they almost always come from the XML feed. This guide explains the most frequent causes and what can be fixed safely.

## What a GTIN is

GTIN (Global Trade Item Number) is the number of the product's barcode. In the feed, it goes in the `g:gtin` tag. A valid GTIN has **8, 12, 13 or 14 digits** and the last one is a **check digit**, calculated from the previous ones. If any digit is wrong, Merchant Center rejects the value.

## Common causes of the error

- **GTIN with a mask:** dots, spaces or dashes in the middle of the number (`789.123...`).
- **Wrong length:** too few or too many zeros, or an internal store code instead of the barcode.
- **Invalid check digit:** the number has the right length but was typed or generated wrong.
- **Empty field** on products that should have a barcode.
- **Malformed XML:** stray HTML in the description, an unescaped `&` and unclosed tags make Merchant Center read the feed wrongly.

## How to fix each case

### Mask and broken XML

Remove dots, spaces and dashes from the GTIN and leave only the digits. In descriptions and titles, remove the HTML and escape `&` as `&amp;`. This can be done in bulk with a tool.

### Invalid check digit

There is no honest automatic fix here: only the manufacturer knows the right number. Check the GTIN on the packaging or in the supplier's catalog. Making up a number that passes validation is worse than leaving the field out, because Google may reject the product for incorrect information.

### Product without a GTIN

If the product really has no barcode (handmade items, made to order, private label), don't fill in `g:gtin`. Send the brand and the manufacturer code (`g:brand` and `g:mpn`) and, when no identifier exists, set `g:identifier_exists` to `no`. Check Google's official documentation for the rules of your category.

## Quick checklist

1. Is the feed well-formed XML?
2. Do the GTINs have 8, 12, 13 or 14 digits, with no mask?
3. Were the GTINs flagged as invalid checked with the manufacturer?
4. Do products without a barcode have no `g:gtin` and the right `identifier_exists`?
5. After fixing, was the feed resubmitted and the status followed in Merchant Center?
